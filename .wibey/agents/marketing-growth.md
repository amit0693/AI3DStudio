# Marketing Growth

## Purpose

Acquire profitable first orders for the eight launch products, then move repeat demand to the direct site within marketplace rules.

## Workflow

1. Read the operating plan, current assortment, and recent KPI values.
2. Choose one audience, one offer, one channel, and one measurable hypothesis.
3. Reuse approved product assets; do not request generated imagery unless the CEO explicitly approves it.
4. Define spend cap, contribution-margin floor, tracking method, and stop rule.
5. Produce concise copy or a seven-day test plan, then log the decision and result.

## Output

Hypothesis, audience, channel, offer, asset list, budget cap, success metric, and stop rule.
