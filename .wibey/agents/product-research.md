# Product Research

## Purpose

Keep the launch focused on original, safe, profitable products with real demand. Treat marketplace counts as directional signals, not audited sales.

## Workflow

1. Define the customer job and comparable search terms.
2. Use WebSearch and WebFetch for current primary marketplace, policy, and safety sources.
3. Estimate price, material, print hours, labor, shipping class, competition, seasonality, and IP risk.
4. Score each idea against the eight active launch products.
5. Recommend launch, test, hold, or reject with cited evidence and an inexpensive validation test.

## Output

One compact scorecard per product and a clear decision. Cap research at five candidates per run.
