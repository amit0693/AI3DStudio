# BayLayer Labs Company Operating System

## Mission and phase

BayLayer Labs sells made-to-order personalization and small-business products, not undifferentiated 3D prints. The current phase is a measured pilot: validate the launch offers, protect cash, and scale only from evidence. Agent roles are invoked for assigned work; they do not run continuously.

## Decision rights

- **Chief Executive:** ranks initiatives, assigns owners, resolves tradeoffs, approves spend and scale.
- **Technology Lead:** owns web/mobile architecture, data, releases and reliability.
- **Marketing & Growth:** owns positioning, listings, content tests and acquisition within approved margins.
- **Brand Design:** owns conversion UX, reusable imagery, accessibility and visual consistency.
- **Product Research:** owns demand evidence, offer scorecards and experiment recommendations.
- **Operations & Supply:** owns vendors, materials, production, packaging and shipping performance.
- **Finance & Unit Economics:** approves prices and validates fully loaded contribution margin.
- **Quality & Verification:** owns test evidence, claim checks, product safety and release gates; it can stop a release.

## Weekly cadence

1. Monday: CEO reads the tracker, selects at most three company priorities, and assigns one accountable owner each.
2. Tuesday–Thursday: owners execute bounded briefs and attach evidence, decisions and blockers to the tracker.
3. Friday: Finance updates unit economics; Quality verifies acceptance criteria; CEO closes, continues or cancels work.
4. Monthly: review product mix, channel profitability, inventory turns and customer evidence; expand only after gates pass.

## Low-token operating rules

- Invoke the smallest relevant role with a narrow objective, named files or data, acceptance criteria and output limit.
- Reuse tracker facts and existing product imagery; do not repeat background research or generate speculative assets.
- Prefer structured deltas: decision, evidence, risk, next action. One owner writes; reviewers comment instead of rewriting.
- Parallelize only independent workstreams. Close every assignment when its artifact and verification evidence exist.

## Supply, packing and shipping

- Pilot inventory budget: $350–$450; full inventory only after demand evidence, approximately $650–$750.
- Use ELEGOO as the verified value baseline: its US store listed standard PLA at $13.99 per 1 kg and a 10 kg option at $99.99 when checked on 2026-08-11. Use Bambu as the premium/backup source; PLA Basic was $19.99 per roll with official bulk/refill offers from $13.99. Treat SUNLU as quote-only until its landed price and a sample spool are verified. Sources: [ELEGOO PLA](https://us.elegoo.com/collections/filaments/products/pla-filament-1-75mm-colored-1kg), [Bambu PLA Basic](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/pla-basic-filament).
- Standardize three box sizes: 8×6×4, 10×8×6 and 14×11×10 inches. Run drop and damage tests with final inserts.
- Buy labels through Pirate Ship. Default to USPS Ground Advantage below 2 lb and compare UPS for larger parcels. Price-check every lane during the current temporary USPS increase; Pirate Ship reports an 8% domestic USPS increase from 2026-04-26 through 2027-01-17. Source: [Pirate Ship 2026 USPS change](https://support.pirateship.com/en/articles/14491291-april-2026-usps-time-limited-price-change).
- Phase 1 website rule: $6.99 flat US shipping below $65 and free direct shipping at $65; use local pickup for bulky planters and B2B samples. Reprice or move to live rates if the packed-order shipping average breaches the margin gate.

## Channel sequence

Launch on the owned site, Etsy and local channels first. Add eBay for functional products, TikTok only when production content is repeatable, and consider Amazon Handmade after 50–100 successfully fulfilled orders.

## Verification gates

- AOV >= $45; direct contribution >= 50%; marketplace contribution >= 40%.
- Print failure <= 8% during pilot and <= 5% afterward; returns or damage <= 3%; on-time shipment >= 95%.
- Verify personalization previews, QR/NFC behavior, prices, lead times, mobile parity, checkout, accessibility and legal/safety copy before launch.
- Calculate margin with material, machine time, failed prints, labor, packaging, platform fees and shipping—not filament alone.
- Add printer capacity only after utilization stays above 65% for four consecutive weeks.
